  • 4 options (A, B, C, D) per question; marking more than one option = unattempted.
  • Paper is bilingual (Hindi & English). If translations differ, the Hindi version is final.
  • Up to 3× the vacancies per category are called to submit documents for the interview.

Marking: +3 / −1 (Score = 3R − W)

A blind guess between 4 options has an expected value of zero (¼ × 3 − ¾ × 1). If you can rule out even one option, guessing becomes worthwhile (+0.33 marks expected). If you can rule out two, it is clearly worth it (+1 mark expected). Only skip questions where you genuinely cannot eliminate anything.

A strong Biology score cannot rescue a weak GS score. With zero wrong answers, 60/150 in GS means 20 correct out of 50; every wrong answer adds 1/3 of a question you must make up. The full mock tests show a section-wise pass/fail check for exactly this reason.

Section A: General Studies (50 Questions, 150 Marks)

This is one-third of the written marks and has its own 40% cut-off. It is almost entirely Madhya Pradesh-specific — generic national GK will not be enough.

Unit 1 — History of MP

  • Prehistoric (Bhimbetka, Hathnora), protohistoric and ancient (Avanti, Mauryas at Sanchi/Vidisha, Guptas at Udayagiri/Eran)
  • Medieval (Paramaras, Chandelas, Kalachuris, Tomars, Malwa Sultanate, Gond kingdoms, Bundelas, Marathas)
  • Modern history, 1857 in MP, freedom movement, tribal history & literature

Unit 2 — Geography of MP

  • Location, rivers, mountains, climate, soils, forests & forest produce
  • Crops, irrigation projects, thermal & renewable power, minerals, population, transport, food processing

Unit 3 — Polity & Economy of MP

  • Governor, CM, Vidhan Sabha, High Court, Lokayukta, secretariat, district & urban administration, Panchayati Raj
  • State commissions; SC/ST Atrocities Act 1989, PESA 1996, Environment Protection Act 1986, MP Govansh Vadh Pratishedh Adhiniyam 2004
  • Economy overview, agriculture, industry, infrastructure, education/health/skills, SDG, EoDB, MPI

Unit 4 — Tribes, Heritage & Folk Culture

  • Tribal spread & constitutional provisions, major tribes & PVTGs, welfare schemes
  • Arts, festivals, dialects, literature, tribal freedom fighters, museums & institutions, folk culture

Unit 5 — Current Affairs & ICT

  • International, national & MP current affairs (focus: last 12 months), MP schemes, personalities & places
  • Computers, ICT, e-governance; basics of AI, ML, cloud, data science, IoT

Notes

  • Pace: 150 questions in 180 minutes = 1.2 minutes per question. Each 50-question practice test has a suggested time of 60 minutes.
  • A provisional answer key is published on www.mppsc.mp.gov.in with 5 days for objections. Questions with no correct option are deleted and full marks are given to everyone; questions with more than one correct option accept all correct options.
  • There is no re-evaluation or re-totalling.
  • Practice tests here are in English. In the real paper, remember the Hindi version prevails if there is a discrepancy — learn key Hindi terms for forensic concepts.
